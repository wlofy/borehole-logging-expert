# Sources and where to read deeper

The references in this skill are written in our own words from the sources
below, plus general practice. No source text is reproduced; consult the original
for exact wording, and the governing standard for any value that becomes a hard
product rule.

## Books

**Look, B. G. — Handbook of Geotechnical Investigation and Design Tables
(Taylor & Francis, 2007).** Australian practice (AS 1726), compact tables.
Most relevant to logging:
- Ch. 1 Site investigation: phases, extent and depth of investigation, sample
  amount and disturbance, quality of an investigation.
- Ch. 2 Soil classification: borelog sequence of terms, field vs final log,
  drilling symbols, water level symbols, particle sizes, USCS, gradings,
  colour, plasticity, Atterberg limits, structure, consistency, density,
  moisture, origin, residual soils.
- Ch. 3 Rock classification: rock description order, field core log,
  weathering, structure, RQD, strength, hardness, defect spacing and
  descriptors, defect codes, rock types. (Note: its core-size table misprints
  HQ; see `03` §2.)
- Ch. 4 Field sampling and testing: sample types and symbols, boring types,
  field test symbols, SPT notation in soil and rock, SPT corrections, CPT,
  DMT, PMT, vane and its correction, DCP.
- Ch. 5 Soil strength from classification and testing: pocket penetrometer
  use and limits, Cu from SPT.
- Ch. 6 Rock strength: rig refusal as a strength indicator, field strength
  evaluation, point load index.
- Ch. 10 Material and testing variability.

**Das, B. M. (ed.) — Geotechnical Engineering Handbook (J. Ross Publishing).**
Broader design reference. Most relevant to logging:
- Ch. 1 Engineering properties of soil (Sivakugan): soil classification, §1.8
  site investigation (SPT, CPT, vane, sample area ratio), §1.10
  instrumentation.
- Ch. 8 Expansive clays (Petry): §8.11 investigations.
- Ch. 10 Site investigation and in-situ tests (Shukla): stages of
  investigation, methods of subsurface investigation and borehole problems
  (caving, base heave, drilling fluid), sampling (area ratio, recovery ratio,
  sampler types), rock coring (core recovery, RQD), geophysics, SPT, CPT, DCPT,
  plate load, field vane, pressuremeter, dilatometer, in-situ CBR and density.

The copy of this handbook in the team's possession came from a file-sharing
site; buy or license a legitimate copy before relying on it commercially.

## Standards (consult the current editions)

ASTM D2488 (visual-manual description), ASTM D2487 (USCS), ASTM D1586 (SPT),
ASTM D1587 (thin-walled tubes), ASTM D2573 (field vane), ASTM D5778 (CPT),
ASTM D6032 (RQD), ASTM D4083 (frozen soils); Canadian Foundation Engineering
Manual (CFEM); AS 1726 (Australia); BS 5930 and BS EN ISO 14688/14689 (UK and
international); ISRM suggested methods (rock description); AGS 4 data format.

## Public articles used for orientation

- SOCOTEC, "Borehole logging and borehole logs" (driller's vs engineer's log,
  drilling parameters, AGS).
- Rocscience, "The critical value of field documentation and lab
  reconciliation" (keep field and final descriptions; audit trail; vendor
  article).
- Queensland TMR, Geotechnical Borehole Logging guide (free; worth reading in
  full).
- FHWA NHI-01-031, Chapter 4 (US subsurface investigation practice).
